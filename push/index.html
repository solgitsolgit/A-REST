Problem & Context

High Academic Workload & Lack of Campus Rest Space

Validating on-demand mobility to relieve physiological fatigue across campus

#01. Space Scarcity

Vanishing Rest Areas in Academic Buildings

Lack of designated rest rooms in campus buildings leads to physical fatigue from sleeping at library desks.

#02. High Travel Friction

Off-Campus Nap Cafés are Inconvenient

Traveling to off-campus rest spots takes 40+ minutes of round-trip walking with costly hourly fees.

#03. Uncontrolled Environment

Noise & Lighting in Libraries & Lounges

Open study spaces prevent controlling light, noise, and temperature, degrading short-term rest quality.
